The apps

Three apps. One idea: less on the screen, more in your life.

Each one does a single thing well and then gets out of the way. They share one look, black, white and a single colour, so they feel like one family on your phone.

yrs.

Your life,
in dots.

Every year you've lived and every year still ahead, one dot each. Zoom from all of history down to a single day, count the visits left with the people you love, and put it on your lock screen so you spend the time on purpose.

Lock screensGrid, Daylight, Hourglass and Stars, redrawn every day.
Seven widgetsLife, Number, Decade, This year, Days, Today, People.
Letters to laterSeal a letter that opens on a future birthday.
Count your wayYears, weeks, days, summers, weekends or full moons.

yrs. music

Your songs,
played beautifully.

An offline player for the music saved on your phone. Songs, Albums and Playlists, nothing else. Tap the playing strip and the cover grows into the player on a spring, with one colour borrowed from the artwork. It has no internet permission at all.

Three wordsSongs is home, with your favourite albums pinned above.

About

Hi. It's just me behind all of this.

There's no company here, no team and no investors. I design, code and ship every yrs. app myself: the icons, the words, the builds, the bugs.

I make the apps I want on my own phone. Most software is built to keep you looking at it a little longer. I'd rather build things that show you what matters and then let you go: how much time you have, the people waiting for you, the songs you love.

Everything is free and stays free. If something is broken, or could be calmer, simpler or more beautiful, tell me. I read every message.

Thanks for being here. — jrypzm
