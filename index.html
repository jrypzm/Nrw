Questions

Good to know.

Is yrs. free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not as an app yet. On iPhone, open the web version in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". It works offline and keeps everything on your phone. A full iPhone app is planned.

What happens to my birthday and notes?

They never leave your phone. The app doesn't even have permission to use the internet. Uninstalling removes everything.

How is "years left" worked out?

From your birthday and a lifespan you choose (80 by default). In settings, five honest questions can adjust it. It's an estimate to reflect on, not a prediction or medical advice.

How do I update?

Download again from this page and install. The new version goes over the old one and keeps everything you've added.

Why does Android warn me when installing?

Android shows a warning for any app installed from outside the Play Store. yrs. is built and signed automatically from its public source code on GitHub.
