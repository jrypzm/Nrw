About

Made by one person.Open to everyone.

yrs. is a small app that shows your life as dots: one for every year you've lived, one for every year ahead, and one for the year you're in, slowly filling.

It isn't a company or a startup. It's an independent project, made by one person in spare time, because I wanted a calm, honest reminder that time is finite, and I couldn't find one I liked. So I built it, and now it's yours too.

  • Free to useNo price, no subscription, no ads. Install it and use all of it.
  • Open source, MITRead every line. Build it yourself, change anything, or take the code and make it your own, your way. Keep the license note, that's all.
  • PrivateNo account, no tracking. The Android app can't even reach the internet. Your birthday stays on your phone.
  • Made in the openIdeas, bugs and pull requests are welcome on GitHub. It gets better when people tell me what they'd change.

Android

The full app, with widgets and a lock screen that keeps itself up to date.

  1. Tap Download. You always get the latest build.
  2. Open yrs.apk from your notifications. If asked, allow installs from your browser.
  3. yrs. isn't on the Play Store yet, so Play Protect may ask. Tap Install anyway. Updates keep your data.
DownloadAndroid 10 or newer

Linux

A fast native app for your desktop. On KDE Plasma it keeps your wallpaper and lock screen up to date.

  1. Download yrs-x86_64.AppImage. One file, about 5 MB.
  2. Make it executable: right-click, Properties, Permissions, Is executable. Or chmod +x it.
  3. Open it. yrs. adds itself to your app launcher, ready to pin to the taskbar.
DownloadFedora, Ubuntu, Arch · Wayland or X11

In your browser

Try yrs. right now, free, with nothing to install. On any phone, tablet or computer.

  1. Open jrypzm.github.io/yrs/web.
  2. Enter your birthday. That's it: there's your life.
  3. Like it? Install it as an app. On iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android or a computer: tap Install. yrs. shows you how when you open it.

See your life in seconds

No download, no account, no sign-up. Open the link on any phone, tablet or computer, enter your birthday, and you're looking at your life.

An app on iPhone, too

Add it to your Home Screen from Safari's Share button and yrs. becomes an app: its own icon, full screen, offline, in the iPhone's own SF Pro. No App Store needed.

Private by design

Everything stays in your browser, on your device. No account, no server, nothing uploaded. Updates arrive the moment they're published.

What a browser can't do

Browsers can't add widgets or change your wallpaper on their own, so you save the lock screen once from Settings. For widgets and a lock screen that updates itself, get the Android app, or the Linux app for your computer.
