# Code of conduct

yrs. is about time, and how little of it there is. Don't waste anyone's.

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, never the person.
- Welcome people who are new; everyone was once.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, of any kind.

Anything that breaks this may be removed, and repeat behaviour may lead to a block. To report something privately, use [private reporting](https://github.com/jrypzm/yrs/security/advisories/new).
