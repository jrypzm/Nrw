# Contributing to yrs.

Thank you for wanting to make yrs. better.

## Ideas and bugs

The best way to help is an issue:

- **[Report a bug](https://github.com/jrypzm/yrs/issues/new?template=bug.yml)**: what happened, what you expected, your phone and version.
- **[Suggest an idea](https://github.com/jrypzm/yrs/issues/new?template=idea.yml)**: what it would let you see or feel about your time.

Every issue is read.

## Pull requests

yrs. is not open source; its code is public to read but [all rights are reserved](LICENSE). Please open an issue and agree the change before writing code. Unrequested pull requests may be closed.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be used in yrs. under the project's license, and that you have the right to submit it.

## What fits yrs.

- **Calm.** Black, white, one accent: the dots. No colours, gradients or badges.
- **Honest.** Numbers are calendar-exact. A heading counts in the unit it shows.
- **Private.** Nothing leaves the phone. No network, accounts or analytics.
- **One idea per screen.** If it needs an explanation, it isn't finished.
